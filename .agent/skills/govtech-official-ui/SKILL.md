# MDIAS — Palette 3 AI / Innovation Dark & Decision-Support Prototype UI/UX Skill (`govtech-official-ui`)

## Purpose
Ensures every page and component of the **Maharashtra Drought & Earth Observation Intelligence Platform (MDIAS)** adheres to a transparent, high-trust **Independent Research & Decision-Support Prototype** framing with the **Palette 3 — AI / Innovation (Purple-Accent Dark)** color system:
- **Framing**: Always present MDIAS honestly as an **Independent Research & Decision-Support Prototype** (`MDIAS — Maharashtra Drought & Earth Observation Intelligence Platform (Decision-Support Prototype)`).
- **No Unverified Official Claims**: Never claim to already be an official Government of Maharashtra portal; do not use `GOVT. OF MAHARASHTRA`, `OFFICIAL TELEMETRY`, `MANTRALAYA SYNC`, `SEOC Directives`, `Cabinet Briefing`, or fake Government Resolution codes (`GR-WCD-2026/...`).
- **Terminology**: Use accurate, research-grade decision-support terms such as `Recommended Mitigation Protocols`, `Regional Summary Matrix`, `Priority District Watchlist`, and `Model Reference ID (SIM-...)`.

## Palette 3 — AI / Innovation (Purple-Accent Dark) Color Architecture

1. **Surfaces & Canvas**:
   - **Background (Primary)**: `#09090B` (`bg-[#09090B]`) — Pure zinc-obsidian canvas (ZERO navy blue `slate-900`/`slate-950`!).
   - **Background (Secondary)**: `#18181B` (`bg-[#18181B]`) — Cards, panels, and primary containers.
   - **Background (Tertiary)**: `#27272A` (`bg-[#27272A]`) — Hover states, selected rows, and inner sub-cards.
   - **Border**: `#3F3F46` (`border-[#3F3F46]`) — Crisp hairline dividers and card outlines.

2. **Typography & Accents**:
   - **Text (Primary)**: `#FAFAFA` (`text-[#FAFAFA]`) — Headings, primary metrics, and high-contrast body text (19.1:1 AAA contrast on `#09090B`).
   - **Text (Secondary)**: `#A1A1AA` (`text-[#A1A1AA]`) — Descriptions, subtitles, and metadata.
   - **Accent (Primary)**: `#A855F7` (`bg-[#A855F7]`, `text-[#A855F7]`, `border-[#A855F7]/40`) — Primary CTAs, interactive highlights, and hero gradients.
   - **Accent (Secondary)**: `#6366F1` (`bg-[#6366F1]`, `text-[#6366F1]`, `border-[#6366F1]/40`) — Secondary badges, hydrological tags, and interactive accents.

3. **Telemetry & Geographic Precision**:
   - All numerical metrics (Soil Moisture `%`, SPI-3M, NDVI anomaly, Riverbed Exposed `%`, GPS coordinates, Model Reference IDs) MUST use `font-mono` (`tabular-nums`) with explicit units.
   - Always use updated Maharashtra district names (`Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar`, `Dharashiv`, `Ahilyanagar`).
